## HCD.15:5 - Archetypal Grounding {#archetypal-grounding}

### HCD.15:5.1 - A constructed continuation arrangement for Engineer-E27 {#a-constructed-continuation-arrangement-for-engineer-e27}

Engineer-E27, `CapabilityDevelopmentTeam-E27`, `ReleaseWork-R9`, and every observation below are **constructed** for this example. The case does not establish that ReleaseWork-R9 occurred, that a provider operated, or that E27 developed. Current engineering, safety, release, employment, and specialist owners must supply their own results for operational use.

The intended later-Work family is independent review of AI-assisted mixed physical/software release claims. The work may require configuration-impact reasoning, challenge of plausible wrong AI output, auditable evidence return, justified action or refusal, and bounded safety and maintenance specialist use. The constructed 2026-Q4 window preserves the current configuration, permitted AI conditions, current sources, critical-error criteria, and specialist-return boundary.

The example stipulates one compatible HCD.14 input: familiar review practice remains supported, while weak independent counter-hypothesis generation in unfamiliar AI-error cases calls for replacing one cue-led correction exercise with action/refusal contrasts and one fresh uncued counter-hypothesis case. That is a revision hypothesis, not evidence that the change was authorized, performed, or effective.

The continuing arrangement is:

| Position | Constructed arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| Preserved | Familiar trace-to-source practice, configuration checking, evidence return, permitted tool access, and required specialist returns remain available under their existing conditions. |
| Triggers | Reopen independent challenge after a material release-Method or configuration change, a changed AI model or assistance rule, a newly observed critical error, failed unfamiliar transfer or delayed performance, changed specialist access, a source contradiction, or expiry of the bounded qualification window. A quarter boundary alone does not reopen it. |
| Practice contribution | On a trigger, use one fresh representative case containing a plausible AI claim and a material configuration variation. Obtain E27's first trace, counter-hypothesis, action or refusal, and specialist return before coaching or AI prompts name the decisive alternative. |
| Feedback and support | Provide criterion-bearing feedback, one contrasting case, current source access, the permitted AI configuration, coaching, and bounded safety or maintenance specialist access. Record help that supplies recognition separately from E27's contribution. |
| Community contribution | A provider review forum may compare anonymized reasoning traces only if facilitation, access, confidentiality, the exact practice question, and a responsible feedback owner are present. Attendance does not count as evidence of E27's capability. |
| Evidence and reassessment | Keep first and corrected attempts, critical errors, source/configuration use, unfamiliar variation, support, burden, and unresolved rivals separate. One successful fresh case can retain the local arrangement provisionally; it cannot establish broad transfer, retention, or causal effect. |

Return `continuing-arrangement-blocked` for this practice contribution to its allocation owner; resume when an agreed allocation covers preparation, the attempt, feedback and comparison. Extra follow-up needs a separately authorized time, assessor, task, and access arrangement. Release, safety, employment, and provider-operation decisions remain elsewhere. |

No result is stipulated. Conditional returns keep the arrangement honest:

| Later observation | Arrangement response |
| --- | --- |
| E27 independently generates and tests a relevant counter-hypothesis on the fresh case while preserving trace and specialist return | Retain this local response provisionally for the tested configuration; do not claim general capability or causal effect. |
| Correct challenge appears only after a coach or AI prompt names the alternative | Independent contribution remains unproved; adapt the cue relation or return to the affected target rather than count completion. |
| The AI configuration or source changes before the attempt | Requalify the task and evidence window; do not interpret the old arrangement as current. |
| Specialist access is absent for a case that requires it | Return the access or service result to its owner; do not infer a human deficit. |
| Traceability or another protected contribution deteriorates | Stop or revise the local change; the intended gain does not compensate for the protected loss. |

### HCD.15:5.2 - A professional credit rule is not yet an arrangement {#a-professional-credit-rule-is-not-yet-an-arrangement}

A professional body may require annual credits. That rule can supply a qualification or re-entry condition, but it does not identify the person's changed later Work, the affected development claim, representative contribution, provider capability, transfer or retention evidence, burden, or response to failure. Use the rule as one external condition and build the HCD arrangement only when those human-development values are recoverable.

## HCD.15:6 - Bias-Annotation {#bias-annotation}

Recency pressure can make every new tool, source, or incident look like a reason to retrain. Ask which exact holder claim and receiving decision the change can affect, then preserve the rest. Conversely, familiarity with a credential or annual schedule can make repeated activity look safer than a claim-sized reassessment.

Community and provider visibility create another bias: participation is easy to count, while independent transfer and critical errors are harder to observe. Keep activity useful as an arrangement contribution without upgrading it to capability evidence.

## HCD.15:8 - Common Anti-Patterns and How to Avoid Them {#common-anti-patterns-and-how-to-avoid-them}

| Anti-pattern | Better move |
| --- | --- |
| “Renew twelve credits every year.” | Tie re-entry to a material qualification, Work, evidence, source, support, or risk condition and name the decision it can change. |
| “The person attended, so capability is current.” | Treat participation as one contribution and obtain evidence for the relied-on human contribution. |
| “Repeat the whole programme after every change.” | Recover the smallest affected question and preserve supported claims and arrangement components. |
| “The community will keep everyone current.” | Name facilitation, access, practice, feedback, provider, and evidence contributions; membership proves none of them by itself. |
| “The plan means continuing development is happening.” | Separate the designed and authorized arrangement from performed development Work and observed result. |
| “More frequent assessment is always safer.” | Use the lightest decision-bearing evidence and stop when added burden or surveillance no longer changes the decision. |

## HCD.15:10 - Rationale {#rationale}

Continuing Human Capability Development is necessary because the capability claim and its later-Work conditions can change after an initial programme. Its proper object is therefore the holder-specific arrangement that relates decision-bearing changes to development contributions and evidence, not a calendar or an assertion of permanent capability.

The smallest affected-question rule preserves supported value and keeps burden proportionate. The explicit result distinctions prevent a designed opportunity, provider activity, participation record, or successful helped attempt from standing in for performed development, later-Work contribution, capability, or effect.

## HCD.15:11 - SoTA-Echoing {#sota-echoing}

**Working question:** how should continuing workplace development remain responsive to changed Work without equating recurring instruction or participation with capability?

**Selected line — adopt and adapt.** Kraiger and Ford's [science of workplace instruction review](https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-orgpsych-012420-060109) is the best-known-line candidate for the evidence spine used here: instructional events, learner activity, learning outcomes, transfer, and job performance remain different, while learner, design, work environment, opportunity, and support condition the result. HCD.15 adapts that line in §§4.1–4.7 by connecting one changed later-Work or evidence condition to a holder-specific continuing arrangement, explicit re-entry, owners, burden, and reassessment. The review does not supply one cadence, intervention, or E27 trajectory.

The serious default is annual-credit, repeat-course, or membership-based continuation. It is cheaper to administer, but it cannot show which development claim changed, whether the relevant contribution was elicited, or whether it survived unfamiliar, delayed, or changed-support conditions. HCD.15 rejects activity count as the capability inference while retaining a credit rule, course, or community as a bounded contribution or external condition when it genuinely changes access or qualification.

Matsuo and Tsukube's [cognitive-apprenticeship review](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijme.2020.100417), Wiese, Kilty, and Bennett's [realist synthesis of supervised workplace learning](https://doi.org/10.1111/medu.13655), Abedini, Abedin, and Zowghi's [review of adult online communities of practice](https://doi.org/10.1111/bjet.13120), and Brooks and colleagues' [community-support study](https://doi.org/10.1002/lrh2.10412) support bounded modeling, coaching, supervision, access, facilitation, platform, and coordination questions. They are contribution and boundary sources, not evidence for a universal provider form, transplanted clinical governance, community effect, or this holder's capability. This comparison changes §§4.4–4.6 and the participation checks in §§5 and 7.

The selected line accepts more case-specific work than counting attendance, but it is no worse on actionability and better on evidential truth, local repair, protected conditions, and safe returns. Reopen the comparison if stronger current workplace-development evidence supplies an equally truthful lower-burden arrangement, or if a changed population, Work family, provider form, support condition, measure, horizon, or serious alternative defeats one of the relied-on contribution claims.
