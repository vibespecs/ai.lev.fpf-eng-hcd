## HCD.17:5 - Archetypal Grounding {#archetypal-grounding}

### HCD.17:5.1 - A constructed E27 cultural-continuation branch {#a-constructed-e27-cultural-continuation-branch}

This hypothetical example concerns CapabilityDevelopmentTeam-E27, its trainers and Engineer-E27. All described participants, records and decisions are stipulated.

The provider group prepares release-review practitioners in the stipulated 2026-Q4 period. The intended learner contribution is to generate and test a configuration counter-hypothesis before accepting plausible wrong AI output, while preserving source tracing, critical-error detection, justified action or refusal and specialist returns. The cultural question concerns how **other trainers take up the teaching practice** used to develop that contribution.

Use the candidate/observed-practice branch for Counter-Hypothesis-First Candidate-E27. Its teaching sequence presents a fresh review case, elicits the learner's response before giving a cue that names the alternative, provides criterion-bearing feedback, and arranges an informative retry. Keep the candidate's status; using its materials does not admit a Method. A lead trainer prepares receiving trainers through the scenario card, paired cases, current sources and coached preparation. The receiving trainers then conduct practice sessions with learners.

The primary cultural claim is that the receiving trainers use this teaching sequence in later sessions. A positive case shows a receiving trainer eliciting the learner's first response before the answer-bearing cue, then providing feedback and the intended retry. A negative case shows the trainer giving the alternative before the learner attempts it, so the session no longer elicits the intended first response. Trainers may use their card and references. The learner may still struggle in a session where the trainer follows the practice correctly; learner success alone also does not show what teaching practice the trainer used.

Keep the teaching materials, preparation, receiving trainer's session, learner's attempts and feedback distinguishable in the account. Permitted AI use, source/configuration checks, critical errors and specialist access remain conditions of the practice. Stipulated authority lets one provider steward change arrangements for this provider group; it grants no authority over release decisions, employment, safety or the wider field.

For the unchanged arrangement, stipulate that session records show receiving trainers using the sequence after combined material-based and coached preparation. Their participation, access and protected conditions remain adequate. Return `continue` for that bounded arrangement: another comparison would take time from useful operation without changing this decision. The records leave preparation by materials alone, long-term retention and wider prevalence unresolved.

Separately, the example may stipulate that E27 produced an uncued counter-hypothesis in a fresh learner attempt while preserving source trace and specialist return. HCD.11 interprets that performance under its conditions. It is not a substitute for the observation of receiving trainers, and it establishes neither broad capability nor the teaching practice's causal effect.

Now consider preparing receiving trainers with fewer coached sessions. The combined-support records do not settle that proposal. A small protected preparation session is authorized and obtainable; its result can change whether to reduce coaching or retain the present support, and that contribution warrants the whole preparation, teaching, observation and feedback burden. Two hypotheses matter to this selected comparison:

| Hypothesis | Discriminating expectation |
| --- | --- |
| H-E27-CARRIER — the card and paired cases suffice for receiving trainers to use the sequence | After self-study, a trainer conducts a fresh teaching task with the card available, eliciting the learner's first response before supplying the alternative, then giving feedback and a suitable retry. |
| H-E27-COACH — coached preparation is still needed for that use | The pre-coaching teaching task departs from the sequence, while an equivalent fresh task after coaching follows it. This observation can support retaining coaching without identifying a causal effect by itself. |

Return a bounded `branch` proposal: preserve the present arrangement, test the reduced preparation with the authorized trainers, and compare their use of the sequence on equivalent fresh teaching tasks before and after coaching. Record actual help, learner-task criteria, critical errors, source trace, specialist return and burden. If the trainer follows the sequence, assess any learner consequence separately. The proposal completes the decision to try the branch; its result remains to be observed.

| Observation | Cultural decision |
| --- | --- |
| Receiving trainers use the sequence after material-based preparation, before coaching | Continue that preparation branch provisionally for the tested group and conditions; long-term retention and wider use remain open. |
| Trainers use the sequence only after coaching | Revise the material-sufficiency claim and retain combined preparation; the card alone has not shown the required contribution. |
| Learners give corrected answers, but trainers still supply the alternative before the first attempt | Preserve any learner practice benefit separately; the intended teaching sequence has not been taken up in those sessions. |
| Burden rises, protected source tracing or specialist return deteriorates, or practice becomes unsafe | Stop, revert or redesign the branch even if more trainers follow the sequence. |
| No authorized later observation is available | Withhold the branch's later receiving-use claim. Retain the independently supported current arrangement and state the missing observation only for the decision that needs it. |

A claim about the receiving trainers' longer-term capability, learners' transfer or retention, or the intervention's causal contribution needs its own compatible basis. The cultural result concerns the stated practice, participants, sessions and conditions.

### HCD.17:5.2 - CDIO edition history is not enacted HCD culture {#cdio-edition-history-is-not-enacted-hcd-culture}

CDIO version history can support bounded claims about formal generation, edition, optional-standard introduction, institutional selection, and publication. For one engineering programme, an HCD.17 enacted-culture claim needs the actual provider and receiving population, an admitted Method and HCD Work or an honest candidate branch, one cultural predicate, the participating subjects and evidence for its period, and the capability limits that matter. A claim of later retention needs the corresponding later evidence; a new intervention needs its own authority and useful receiving question.

When those values are absent, return the supported edition or institutional history and the missing enacted-culture evidence. Do not infer “CDIO culture,” prevalence, retention, capability, or effectiveness from the standard or curriculum label.

### HCD.17:5.3 - Community activity is one contribution, not the cultural result {#community-activity-is-one-contribution-not-the-cultural-result}

An online community can supply access, experience exchange, facilitation, coordination, and professional support. Those contributions may participate in a transmission or recognition hypothesis. Membership, posts, platform availability, or a favourable survey do not establish the cultural predicate, individual capability, long-term retention, or causal effect. Use available evidence for the named receiving or later-practice claim, withhold unsupported stronger claims, and obtain another observation only when its receiving contribution warrants the feasible work.

## HCD.17:6 - Bias-Annotation {#bias-annotation}

Institutional prestige and visible carriers make formal editions, standards, schools, repositories, and events easy proxies for culture. Recover the exact value, participants, predicate, positive and negative cases, and available evidence before relying on the label. Later retention or tested-change claims need the observations they assert.

Success in one provider slice creates a scale bias: local receiving use is retold as organizational or field adoption. Preserve the population, period, authority, and unobserved links. Conversely, absence of a field-wide study does not block an honestly bounded current account, continuation, or useful local probe.

## HCD.17:8 - Common Anti-Patterns and How to Avoid Them {#common-anti-patterns-and-how-to-avoid-them}

| Anti-pattern | Better move |
| --- | --- |
| “The standard was adopted, so the culture changed.” | Separate institutional selection and publication from receiving HCD Work, the cultural predicate, later observation, and consequence. |
| “Everyone attended the rollout.” | Treat attendance as one Work or evidence fact; test the named receiving, selection, recognition, memory, retention, or loss predicate. |
| “The cards were used, so the candidate Method was enacted.” | Preserve candidate status and independently establish Method admission and actual enactment before using that branch. |
| “Capability improved, so transmission occurred.” | Assess human capability and the cultural predicate separately; open causal evaluation only when the decision relies on the link. |
| “One team result proves field adoption.” | Keep population, place, period, predicate, authority, and wider gaps visible. |
| “Change the carrier and see whether activity rises.” | Compare credible explanations and use the named cultural predicate. Select a discriminating observation only when it can improve the receiving decision enough to warrant its feasible work. |

## HCD.17:10 - Rationale {#rationale}

Culture becomes actionable when a bounded account of one value and its cultural relation supports a continuation decision. A later observation is useful when it can change that decision or a warranted claim, not because an account has an unfilled slot. HCD.17 adds the provider, learner, practitioner, development-Work, human-capability, and later-Work distinctions needed for this HCD question after C.36 is recognized.

The truthful subject branch permits learning from candidate and source-described practice without laundering Method status. Separating carriers, authority, cultural claim, capability consequence, rival hypotheses, and causal reliance lets a team improve one relation while leaving unproved links open.

## HCD.17:11 - SoTA-Echoing {#sota-echoing}

**Working question:** how should providers and practitioners deliberately continue or change one HCD cultural relation without treating a school, carrier, event, or formal adoption as the culture?

**Selected line — adopt and adapt.** Current C.36 supplies the transdisciplinary move: begin with a bounded practice and the cultural relations supported by the available basis, then continue or select a useful change within evidence and authority. Singh and colleagues' [review beyond “social learning”](https://doi.org/10.1098/rstb.2020.0050) distinguishes several transmission processes and warns that individual acquisition does not prove population continuation. HCD.17 adapts that evidence split with truthful HCD Method/candidate branches, provider and learner Work, and a separate human-capability consequence. The review supplies no HCD intervention recipe, population capability, or E27 result; a new discriminating inquiry earns its place from the actual receiving use.

The serious default is rollout culture: publish a curriculum or standard, train people, count participation, and call later activity adoption. It is administratively cheap but cannot distinguish carrier presence, receiving use, recognition, selection, memory, retention, capability, or causal effect. HCD.17 rejects that compression while retaining each supported publication, institutional-selection, teaching, participation, or community fact under its actual boundary.

Abedini, Abedin, and Zowghi's [review of adult online communities of practice](https://doi.org/10.1111/bjet.13120) and Brooks and colleagues' [community-support study](https://doi.org/10.1002/lrh2.10412) support facilitation, platform, coordination, access, experience exchange and participation as candidate contributions. They do not establish capability gain or cultural continuation. The [CDIO version history](https://cdio.org/content/version-history-and-references) is a necessary explicit comparator for formal generation and institutional-selection claims; official status supplies no evidence of receiving enactment, population retention, or effectiveness. These limits change §§4.3–4.8 and the countercases in §5.

Current ME.17 is the strongest nearby reusable Method but governs Method-Engineering culture. HCD.17 consumes its status, predicate, hypothesis, authorization and interval discipline while retaining the HCD-specific action and consequence. OCE.10 governs organization-side participation and target working culture when organization change is current. The selected HCD line costs more than label counting but is better on falsifiability, local authority, harm prevention, and truthful capability boundaries.

Reopen the comparison when a direct source changes the relevant transmission or community claim, when stronger HCD cultural-intervention evidence changes the predicate or probe, when the population, Method status, provider, support, interval, authority, intended consequence, or serious alternative changes, or when another practice returns the same truth and repair value at lower effort.
