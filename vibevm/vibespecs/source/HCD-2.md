## HCD.2:5 - Archetypal Grounding {#archetypal-grounding}

### HCD.2:5.1 - Constructed Personal Case: Learner-FP6 {#constructed-personal-case-learner-fp6}

`Learner-FP6` is a constructed pedagogical holder. The stipulated entry attempt shows that this person can build a device model but confuses a flow unit, the recipient of the result, and the organizational permission needed for the proposed action. The intended later-Work outputs, mandatory tasks, 340 learner-hour limit, available teacher and worked examples, and protected critical errors are held fixed for this comparison. No observation of actual learning is claimed.

Two six-hour candidates replace harder optional cases rather than add time:

| Candidate | Finite change and intended contribution | Burden, dependence, and evidence gap |
| --- | --- | --- |
| focused preparation | Allocate two hours to selecting the result recipient, two to checking the required organizational permission and tracing consequences for other systems, and two to checking flow units and constraints before the next whole task. The mandatory tasks, later variation, and final result remain unchanged. | Uses an explicit prerequisite route and keeps teacher feedback inside the existing arrangement. It may isolate the three weaknesses clearly, but the comparison still needs a changed-condition whole-task attempt to show whether the contributions combine. |
| supported whole-task preparation | Use the same six learner hours for a complete decision task in which the person must calculate with the flow unit, address the right recipient, and obtain or respect the required permission. A worked example and bounded teacher questions support the first attempt; a changed case then removes answer-revealing help. | Tests integration earlier but may conceal which prerequisite failed. Selecting, demonstrating, and reviewing the extra case consumes teacher preparation and feedback Work that must fit an established capacity or be priced separately. |

HCD.2 returns two comparable descriptions, the common output and evidence plan, and the unresolved provider-load and changed-condition evidence. It does not choose between them. An independent attempt showing three separable errors would strengthen focused preparation; success on the components but failure to combine them would strengthen the whole-task candidate. Either observation reopens only the affected comparison claims.

### HCD.2:5.2 - Unknown Audience: FoundationsProgramme A, B, and C {#unknown-audience-foundationsprogramme-a-b-and-c}

A learning-product author has two intended outcomes: checked performance of complete selected work tasks, and lower effort to learn a previously untaught Method while preserving correctness. These are design aims, not observed gains. The author has an audience hypothesis, three 340-hour compositions, and no named learners. A allocates 202/96/42 hours to general/domain/joint application, B allocates 88/204/48, and C allocates 136/136/68. C is described in more detail; that detail does not establish dominance.

The HCD.2 boundary returns a learning-product design question to HCD.28: preserve the two outputs, audience assumptions, three alternatives, learner and teacher resource coordinates, support assumptions, and individualization questions. Use C's existing material and develop only the smallest missing A/B fragment for one closed task family with the same output, entry assumptions, representative attempt, feedback, retry, access, and resource account. Do not fabricate one future learner or require three complete courses before comparison becomes useful.

If the learner budget falls from 340 to 204 hours while both outputs remain fixed, none of the three original candidates is launchable as described. Multiplying every activity by 0.6 or hiding reading and practice outside the budget does not close the task. The return asks for a new candidate that identifies the removed or replaced 136 hours and preserves the mechanisms needed for the outputs. The separate 240-hour teacher calculation does not replace learner time or establish a competent available service. A 170-hour ThinkingFoundations-only offer answers a narrower output question and therefore is not an improved version of the same candidate set.

### HCD.2:5.3 - One Feasible Candidate Is Not a Winner {#one-feasible-candidate-is-not-a-winner}

A named person has a qualified near-term engineering task and strong entry evidence. Only one candidate currently fits the deadline and available project access; a second candidate depends on an unavailable specialist. HCD.2 may return the feasible candidate, its finite comparison with the current configuration, and the exact specialist result that blocks the other candidate. It does not invent a weak rival, call the feasible programme universally best, or infer that the programme will work. A later provider result, deadline change, or changed task reopens the bounded comparison.

## HCD.2:6 - Bias-Annotation {#bias-annotation}

Lenses: **Gov**, **Arch**, **Onto/Epist**, **Prag**, **Did**. Scope: personal human-capability programme composition and comparison under qualified later-Work, participation, support, and evidence conditions.

| Recurring bias | Likely drift | Repair |
| --- | --- | --- |
| course-solution bias | The requested course or familiar programme is treated as the answer before later Work and baseline are recovered. | Begin from the holder, complete work tasks, current configuration, and supplied development stake. |
| detailed-candidate bias | The most documented candidate appears superior to sketches. | Compare one minimal closed-task fragment on the same positions before expanding the candidates. |
| topic-count bias | Coverage or PatternID count substitutes for contribution to later Work. | Trace every included element to task closure, a prerequisite, a support contribution, an evidence need, or a future option. |
| role-average bias | Audience or role assumptions become one person's current profile. | Require compatible same-holder evidence or issue the learning-product design return. |
| support invisibility | Teacher, assessor, specialist, AI, access, and preparation are assumed free and available. | Record access allocations, actual availability, peak load, and missing enablement separately. |
| assistance attribution | Helped output is treated as independent human capability or learning. | Preserve permitted, available, received, independent, changed-condition, and delayed evidence states. |
| sunk-curriculum bias | Existing content is retained because it was expensive to create. | Compare the finite contribution and displacement of retaining it under the current task and budget. |
| technique-brand bias | A pedagogy label is assumed to work across holders and tasks. | State its proposed mechanism, source limit, required observation, and serious alternative. |

## HCD.2:8 - Common Anti-Patterns and How to Avoid Them {#common-anti-patterns-and-how-to-avoid-them}

| Anti-pattern | Why it fails | Repair |
| --- | --- | --- |
| “Give everyone programme C” | Detail and a broad audience description do not establish personal fit. | Keep C as one design candidate; obtain same-holder inputs before personal comparison. |
| “This programme covers more topics” | Topic count says nothing about task closure, displacement, support, or evidence. | Compare contributions to complete later-Work results and protected errors. |
| “Scale 340 hours to 204” | Uniform scaling can remove preparation, attempts, feedback, or correction that makes the output plausible. | Name every removed or replaced hour and show how the required mechanisms and outputs remain available. |
| “Teacher hours make it feasible” | A time calculation does not establish competence, access, scheduling, or operation at peak load. | Obtain the HCD.7 support result or return the exact missing contribution to HCD.8 for obtain-or-build comparison and the preparation or operating test the selected route needs. |
| “AI support means the person knows it” | Assisted performance can differ from later unassisted performance. | Allocate the human checking contribution and preserve actual help in every observation. |
| “Every component is a prerequisite” | A printed sequence turns legitimate same-task references and specialists into unnecessary memorization or gates. | Separate before-practice prerequisites from simultaneous complements and supports. |
| “One feasible candidate dominates” | Missing rivals or evidence do not create a supported dominance relation. | Return the feasible description and the exact comparison gap. |
| “HCD.2 chooses and launches” | Comparison is collapsed with preference, authority, commitment, and execution. | Pass the comparison to the applicable decision Method and authorized owner. |
| “The course author is the learner” | Recipient and holder are silently merged. | Return a class-level design question now and reopen personal comparison when a named learner supplies compatible evidence. |

## HCD.2:10 - Rationale {#rationale}

Development is configuration-relative. What one programme adds depends on what this person can already contribute, what support is actually available, which complete tasks matter, and which protected errors cannot be traded away. Starting from an empty curriculum exaggerates every addition; starting from a role average fabricates the holder.

Whole tasks reveal coordination and applicability, while claim-sized contribution allocation prevents the whole-task label from hiding references, AI, specialists, or prerequisites. Comparing finite changes keeps resources and displaced work visible. Separating comparison from choice protects preference, authority, commitment, launch, and evidence of effect as their own questions.

## HCD.2:11 - SoTA-Echoing {#sota-echoing}

The working question is: **How should a practitioner compose and compare a development programme for one person's consequential later Work without reducing it to topics or promising an unobserved effect?**

The selected best-known line combines sufficiently whole complex-task practice with expertise-sensitive support, explicit correction and retesting where those mechanisms fit, and configuration-relative finite comparison. The maintained [4C/ID model](https://www.4cid.org/about/) and its [fourth-edition presentation](https://www.4cid.org/readings/books/) supply the serious complex-task design candidate: whole learning tasks, support for non-routine reasoning, timely procedural information, and additional practice for recurrent parts. Paas and van Merriënboer's 2020 review ([`COG-05`](https://doi.org/10.1177/0963721420922183)) supports changing guidance with prior knowledge and task complexity. Winget and Persky's 2022 review ([`COG-06`](https://doi.org/10.5688/ajpe8906)) supplies the bounded formative-assessment, corrective-activity, retest, and threshold arrangement. HCD.2 adapts these contributions into §§4.2–4.3; none establishes later professional performance or the effect of a local programme.

The serious ordinary alternative is a syllabus-first or branded-programme comparison: list content and hours, match them to a role profile, then prefer the most complete or familiar package. It is cheaper to describe, but it hides complete-task closure, same-holder starting evidence, contribution allocations, provider dependence, displaced work, and unequal detail. HCD.2 rejects topic count and programme labels as the comparison basis, while retaining a syllabus as one carrier of a candidate once its contribution is made explicit.

`C.11.CRC` supplies the internal finite-comparison grammar used in §4.4. The HCD evidence chain keeps intended instruction, supported attempts, independent performance, transfer, and retention in their direct pattern owners; HCD.2 carries only the expectations and evidence needs used by the comparison. Bastani and colleagues' 2025 school-mathematics trial ([`AI-04`](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2422633122)) is counterexample evidence that improved AI-assisted practice can fail to improve later unassisted performance; its population and task do not supply an adult workplace effect size or a universal AI prohibition. These limits produce the separate evidence states in §§4.3–4.6 rather than a promised causal programme effect.

This comparison adds design and evidence work, but it is non-dominated for the stated question because it preserves complete-task usefulness, personal fit, support dependence, finite burden, and uncertainty that the cheaper syllabus-first alternative loses. Reopen the selected line if a stronger source changes the complex-task, support, correction, AI-dependence, or transfer boundary, or if representative use shows that the same decision values can be preserved with materially less effort.
