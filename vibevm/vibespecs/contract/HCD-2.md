> **Type:** Human Capability Development practitioner programme-composition pattern
> **Status:** Stable

## HCD.2:0 - Use This When {#use-this-when}

Use this pattern when one named person has a recoverable later-Work demand and a practitioner, manager, or that person needs several feasible ways to develop the required contributions. Begin with the person's current mastered and supported configuration, the complete later-Work tasks, and the conditions of participation; do not begin with a topic list or a preferred course.

The first useful result is a **programme-comparison account**: comparable descriptions of the actual programme candidates, their intended task closure, contribution and access allocations, burdens, uncertainties, evidence needs, applicability conditions, and the smallest change that would reopen the comparison. If only one candidate remains feasible, describe that candidate and the missing comparison rather than inventing a rival or calling it dominant. A separate authorized choice consumes the comparison.

The practical gain is a programme that can be inspected as a finite change from what the person can already do with available support. The practitioner can see whether a proposed component completes a usable contribution, preserves a prerequisite, adds a future option, or merely displaces practice that another required result still needs.

Do not use HCD.2 to design a universal course for an unknown audience, diagnose a person, choose on behalf of the decision owner, promise learning, or establish provider competence. When the current recipient is a learning-product author who does not yet know the learners, return the outputs, audience assumptions, existing alternatives, resource boundary, and individualization questions to [HCD.28](spec://ai.lev/fpf-eng-hcd/contract/HCD-28#root). It constructs and compares the whole learning product; its conditional variants and requirements can then supply this personal comparison. A named person's compatible Work, entry performance, support, and participation conditions are the retry inputs for personal comparison.

## HCD.2:0.1 - Working Terms {#working-terms}

| Name in this pattern | What it denotes |
| --- | --- |
| human holder | The one identified person whose development is being considered. A course audience, job family, cohort, or average participant is not that person. |
| current mastered and supported configuration | The contributions the person can currently make under stated conditions, together with references, AI, tools, specialists, and other supports that are actually available. Unsupported assumptions about current capability or access do not belong in the baseline. |
| capability-development programme | A bounded arrangement of preparation, representative attempts, feedback, correction, retries, access, and later checks intended to help the person make specified contributions in later Work. It is not the learning product that may carry parts of the arrangement and does not establish that development occurred. |
| complete work task | A representative later-Work task whose required result, critical errors, human contributions, supporting contributions, and stopping conditions are sufficiently present to guide programme composition. |
| contribution allocation | A statement of what the person must recognize or perform, what may be retrieved from a reference, what controlled AI help may supply, and what a specialist or other provider must contribute. Several allocations may coexist in one task. |
| access allocation | The tools, sources, people, environments, permissions, timing, and support conditions that a programme assumes or must obtain. Intended help, permitted help, actual availability, and help received in an attempt remain different facts. |
| comparable programme description | One candidate described against the same holder, later-Work outputs, protected errors, horizon, evidence posture, and resource coordinates as the other candidates. |
| learning-product design return | A positive return to HCD.28 for an author who has audience hypotheses but no personal holder evidence. It preserves the product question; HCD.28 returns candidate variants and requirements, while HCD.2 still needs the relevant personal evidence. |

The programme, its description, a later decision to adopt it, the Work that enacts it, and observed human development are different objects. HCD.2 composes and compares descriptions; it does not make the later events true.

## HCD.2:1 - Problem Frame {#problem-frame}

A person may need to recognize when a professional Method applies, retrieve a precise rule, perform a calculation, challenge an AI proposal, and obtain a specialist result in one piece of later Work. A syllabus can mention every topic and still fail to make that combination attainable. Conversely, requiring the person to memorize every detail can waste scarce time and remove useful support already present in the Work.

Programme candidates start from different baselines. One person may already perform the routine operation but miss a hazardous mismatch. Another may need a prerequisite before a whole task is useful. A third may be capable with a reference and specialist return but not under unsupported conditions. Time, participation, project access, teacher capacity, and the cost of later checking can change which programme is feasible.

The practitioner therefore needs to compose finite alternatives around complete work tasks, make their human and external contributions visible, and compare them without treating detail, duration, novelty, or a technique label as evidence of superiority.

## HCD.2:2 - Problem {#problem}

The common shortcut is to translate a demand into topics, allocate hours, and call the result a programme. This hides whether the pieces combine in representative Work, whether practice and correction fit inside the budget, and whether the assumed teacher, data, AI, specialist, or project access exists.

A second shortcut starts with one polished programme and compares every rival with its documentation rather than with the same required result. The detailed candidate then appears to dominate because its assumptions and costs are visible while the others remain sketches.

A third shortcut assigns an audience profile to every future learner. It converts a course-design hypothesis into a personal diagnosis and can recommend development without same-holder evidence. The practitioner needs a useful return for both situations: a personal comparison when compatible inputs exist, and a precise learning-product design question when they do not.

## HCD.2:3 - Forces {#forces}

- Complete tasks expose coordination and critical errors, while some prerequisites need preparation before meaningful whole-task practice.
- Internalized performance can reduce access delay, while reliable references, tools, AI, and specialists can be legitimate parts of later Work.
- A detailed programme is easier to inspect, while unequal detail is not evidence that it contributes more.
- Personalization can remove redundant work, while role labels and audience averages do not establish one person's starting capability.
- A finite time budget forces displacement, while hidden reading, preparation, provider Work, and retries are easy to leave outside the count.
- Early supported performance can enable practice, while it does not establish later independent, changed-condition, or delayed performance.
- One candidate may be the only feasible option now, while feasibility alone does not establish dominance, effectiveness, or a final choice.
- Programme design can reveal missing access or provider capability, while the description does not make that support available.
- Future options can be valuable, while speculative option value must not replace the required near-term work result.

## HCD.2:4 - Solution {#solution}

Compose each candidate as a finite change from the person's current mastered and supported configuration. Keep complete later-Work tasks, human and external contributions, practice and correction, resources, evidence limits, and future options visible on one comparison basis. Return the comparison without making the final choice.

### HCD.2:4.1 - Bind the Personal Comparison {#bind-the-personal-comparison}

Name:

1. the exact human holder and the basis for their participation;
2. the receiving decision, decision owner, later-Work family, configuration, horizon, and protected result or critical-error conditions;
3. a compatible HCD.1 demand account or equivalent qualified later-Work input;
4. the current performance evidence, supported contributions, unresolved gaps, and any HCD.3 target or HCD.4 profile that the comparison actually uses;
5. the professional Methods, target-domain criteria, source access, tools, AI posture, specialists, provider contributions, environment, authority, and time that are available or still assumed; and
6. the development stake that the candidates must address without silently changing it.

HCD.1, HCD.3, and HCD.4 can supply these inputs, but their PatternIDs are not ritual prerequisites. Compatible direct evidence is enough for the positions the current comparison consumes. Use diagnosis only when rival causes would change the programme.

If personal evidence, participation, later Work, or support conditions are missing, stop the personal comparison at the affected claim. For an unknown audience, issue the learning-product design return to HCD.28. When a product variant comes back for a named person, test its entry assumptions, intended contribution, assistance and burden against that person’s compatible evidence before using it as a programme candidate. For a missing target-domain Method or correctness criterion, return the exact qualified outside-owner question. If no candidate can make the supplied development stake feasible, return that constraint and the surviving alternatives to the separate navigation or target decision rather than weakening the stake inside HCD.2.

### HCD.2:4.2 - Compose Task-Closing Candidates {#compose-task-closing-candidates}

1. **Select representative complete tasks.** State the required result, important variants, critical errors, Work products, conditions, and stopping rules. A topic or Method name is only a discovery cue.
2. **Decompose only far enough to allocate contributions.** For every material contribution, state what the person must recognize and perform, what may be retrieved from a current reference, what controlled AI help may supply, and what requires a specialist or other provider. Keep the human check required for assisted output explicit.
3. **Separate prerequisites from simultaneous complements.** A prerequisite must be available before the relevant practice can proceed. A reference, tool, or specialist contribution may instead remain part of the same later Work. Do not teach every complement as if it had to become unaided personal performance.
4. **Construct finite changes from the baseline.** Add, substitute, remove, or retain programme elements relative to the person's current configuration. Explain which task combination each change is intended to close, which future option it opens, and which existing contribution it might displace or weaken.
5. **Include the complete development loop.** Account for preparation, demonstrations or explanations where useful, representative attempts, feedback, correction, retries, changed-condition or later checks, and the access and provider contributions that make those activities possible.
6. **Keep downstream elaboration explicit.** HCD.5 can select the development Methods, HCD.6 can design the practice tasks, HCD.7 can establish available support, HCD.8 can compare obtaining or building a missing contribution and establish the preparation or operating test the selected route needs, and HCD.9–HCD.10 can specify practice and progression. HCD.2 needs enough of those contributions to make a candidate comparable; it does not pretend that an unperformed elaboration already exists.

Recover the relevant vertical within each complete task, using FPF B.1.5.EW when the connections are unclear. Ask what the person or available contributors must perform together while the encompassing work is under way. Adequate performance of isolated constituents can leave their coordination unavailable. Keep already attained contributions and include development only for the missing or insufficiently transferable ones. Include the variety of constituent Methods that the intended task variants require; a single rehearsed path may leave the person unable to choose a different continuation. Check shared time, attention, equipment and assistance as a combined demand rather than counting the same available resource separately for every part.

For example, in a stipulated dance case, strength and recall of the figure are adequate but balance during rotation is not. A programme adds suitable rotational-coordination practice and return to the figure under varied relevant conditions; it need not repeat the already mastered strength and recall work. HCD.3 distinguishes that gap from other causes, HCD.6 designs the practice and HCD.10 distributes its variation. A domain practitioner supplies the exercise and performance criterion.

### HCD.2:4.3 - Make the Descriptions Comparable {#make-the-descriptions-comparable}

Describe every candidate on the same decision-bearing positions:

| Comparison position | Required content |
| --- | --- |
| holder and use | Human holder, decision owner, participation basis, later-Work family, configuration, horizon, and receiving decision. |
| current configuration | Supported current contributions, protected strengths, unavailable values, uncertainty, and evidence window. |
| intended task closure | Complete tasks and outputs the programme is intended to make attainable, including protected errors and variants. Keep intended attainability separate from observed learning. |
| contribution allocation | Human recognition and performance, reference use, controlled AI help and human checking, specialist returns, and other provider contributions. |
| preparation and sequence | True prerequisites, simultaneous complements, dependency reasons, and any replaceable or optional ordering. Printed topic order is not a capability hierarchy. |
| practice and evidence plan | Representative attempts, permitted help, feedback, correction, retries, changed-condition checks, delayed checks, and the observation each supports. |
| access and provider basis | Actual and assumed sources, tools, AI, people, environments, permissions, capacity, peak load, and missing enablement. |
| resources and displacement | Learner time, provider time, preparation, assessment, transition, tool or material burden, and the useful activity displaced by the change. |
| uncertainty and source limits | Evidence for each expected contribution, unsupported effect claims, transfer and retention limits, and the observation that could change the estimate. |
| continuation | Future options opened, preserved or closed; applicability conditions; exact constraint or evidence gap; and smallest change that reopens the comparison. |

Unequal detail is itself a comparison gap. Develop only the smallest missing fragment needed to compare the same task, output, conditions, support, and resource coordinates. Do not require complete competing courses when one closed task family can discriminate the alternatives.

### HCD.2:4.4 - Compare the Finite Programme Changes {#compare-the-finite-programme-changes}

Use `C.11.CRC` to compare each realizable candidate configuration with the person's current configuration. Name the baseline, finite addition or substitution, candidate configuration, horizon, scenarios, result and resource coordinates, interactions, uncertainty, transition, reversibility, and option effects.

For HCD.2, the decision-bearing coordinates normally include:

- closure of the required later-Work outputs and protected critical-error conditions;
- learner, teacher, assessor, specialist, tool, and transition burden;
- dependence on references, AI, cues, project access, authority, and provider availability;
- preservation of current strengths and practice needed by other contributions;
- the qualified expectation and evidence plan for supported, independent, changed-condition, transfer, and delayed performance; and
- future development options and the cost of reopening a closed route.

Use `dominated`, `non-dominated`, `beneficial`, `harmful`, or `indeterminate` only for the candidates, coordinates, horizon, conditions, and evidence actually compared. A programme is not non-dominated merely because it was generated, and one feasible candidate is not dominant merely because no rival is ready. HCD.2 supplies the comparison claim; `C.11` supplies any later bounded choice from the complete choice inputs.

### HCD.2:4.5 - Return the Programme-Comparison Account {#return-the-programme-comparison-account}

Return one account containing:

| Result position | Required content |
| --- | --- |
| comparison boundary | Holder, later Work, supplied development stake, decision owner, candidates, horizon, conditions, participation, evidence window, and direct outside owners. |
| candidate descriptions | The comparable positions in §4.3 for every actual candidate, including any one candidate that remains feasible. |
| comparison claims | Supported relative contribution, burden, dependence, uncertainty, displacement, and option claims; any dominance relation is explicitly bounded. |
| access allocations | Intended human, reference, AI, specialist, provider, environment, permission, and timing contributions, with actual availability distinguished from assumption. |
| evidence and gaps | Sources and observations supporting each claim, missing values, unsupported stronger claims, and the conclusion each gap blocks. |
| disposition | `comparable candidates`, `one feasible candidate with comparison gap`, `current candidate set infeasible`, `personal comparison blocked with the exact missing input and its receiving owner`, or `learning-product design return for an unknown audience`. These are readable HCD.2 dispositions, not a final programme choice. |
| continuation | The smallest comparable fragment, provider result, personal observation, budget change, target decision, or other condition that permits the next comparison or choice. |

When a final choice is requested, pass this account to the authorized decision owner and the applicable decision Method. Preserve the option set, preferences, belief state, outcome model, protected conditions, probe worth, and authority required there. Do not turn the HCD.2 comparison into authorization, commitment, launch, or proof of effect.

### HCD.2:4.6 - Recognition and Assurance {#recognition-and-assurance}

**Recognition.** Use HCD.2 when the live question sounds like “Which way could this person become able to do the whole job?” and the alternatives differ in preparation, practice, support, time, or future options. The smallest useful result may be one comparable task fragment rather than a complete curriculum.

**Assurance.** Stronger reliance needs same-holder and condition-compatible inputs, qualified target-domain tasks and error criteria, credible provider and access claims, complete resource coordinates, and explicit evidence limits. A programme can be compared before it is chosen or run. Effectiveness, transfer, retention, provider operation, and personal development require their own observations.

### HCD.2:4.7 - What Changes in Practice {#what-changes-in-practice}

A practitioner stops asking, “Which course covers all the topics?” and asks, “Which finite programme change closes this person's complete later-Work tasks, with what human and external contributions, at what burden, and what evidence is still missing?” A detailed course becomes one candidate; an unknown audience receives a design question rather than an invented personal profile.

## HCD.2:7 - Conformance Checklist {#conformance-checklist}

- [ ] One exact human holder is named for a personal comparison; an unknown audience receives a learning-product design return rather than an invented profile.
- [ ] The later-Work family, supplied development stake, protected outputs and errors, configuration, horizon, participation, evidence window, decision owner, and outside owners are explicit.
- [ ] The baseline contains only supported current human and external contributions under stated conditions.
- [ ] Every programme candidate closes or tests a recognizable complete-task combination rather than merely covering topics.
- [ ] Human recognition and performance, reference retrieval, controlled AI help and checking, specialist returns, and other provider contributions are allocated explicitly.
- [ ] True prerequisites are distinguished from supports and complements used during the same later Work.
- [ ] Preparation, representative attempts, feedback, correction, retries, changed-condition or delayed checks, and provider/access work are inside the candidate account.
- [ ] Candidates use the same decision-bearing output, error, burden, support, horizon, uncertainty, and future-option positions; unequal detail is reported as a gap.
- [ ] Learner, teacher, assessor, specialist, transition, tool, material, and displaced-work burdens are visible where they change the comparison.
- [ ] Supported, independent, changed-condition, transfer, and retention claims remain distinct, and unobserved gains remain hypotheses.
- [ ] Any dominance wording is bounded to the candidates, coordinates, horizon, conditions, and evidence actually compared.
- [ ] The result contains a usable disposition and smallest reopen condition but no final choice, authorization, launch, provider-operation, or effectiveness claim.

## HCD.2:9 - Consequences {#consequences}

The practitioner gains a comparison that connects development activities to complete later Work, exposes external support before commitment, and preserves uncertainty without becoming silent. A small comparable fragment can be enough to improve a decision, so the method does not require building several complete courses first.

The cost is explicit composition work. Human and provider time, access, transitions, retries, displaced practice, and later evidence can make an attractive programme infeasible. Some requests return to learning-product design, provider enablement, target-domain owners, or a separate target decision rather than yielding a personal recommendation.

## HCD.2:12 - Relations {#relations}

- **`HCD.1`:** can supply the compatible human capability-demand account and later-Work boundary; programme composition does not alter that demand.
- **`HCD.3`:** can supply a qualified human target or non-training return when rival causes change the programme; diagnosis is not mandatory otherwise.
- **`HCD.4`:** can supply the current/target claim comparison, protected strengths, priorities, and trade-offs; a profile does not generate programme alternatives.
- **`HCD.5`:** selects human-development Methods for the target contribution and evidence limits exposed by a candidate.
- **`HCD.6`:** designs representative practice tasks, permitted help, variations, and result criteria needed by the selected Method.
- **`HCD.7`:** establishes which provider, source, tool, AI, access, and human-check contributions are actually available.
- **`HCD.8`:** compares obtaining or building an exact unavailable contribution and establishes the preparation or operating test the selected route needs; already established usable support can be arranged directly through HCD.7.
- **`HCD.9` and `HCD.10`:** carry out focused practice and change its variation, spacing, interleaving, challenge, or help while preserving attempt history.
- **`HCD.11`–`HCD.13`:** provide compatible observed performance, transfer, retention, and support-dependence evidence; the programme description does not manufacture those observations.
- **`HCD.14`:** revises only the programme assumptions and parts affected by compatible evidence.
- **`C.11.CRC`:** supplies the finite configuration-relative comparison claim. **`C.11`** separately supplies a bounded choice when its preference, belief, outcome, probe, and authority inputs exist.
- **PSD and other advice Methods:** may recommend a programme to a named recipient from qualified inputs; advice does not compose the missing programme or make the choice for its owner.
- **HCD.28:** constructs and compares a whole learning product for a future audience, using HCD.5–8 and other applicable contributions. HCD.2 returns the unknown-audience question there and consumes suitable variants only with compatible personal evidence.
- **Learning-product and target-domain practices:** supply applicable instructional/carrier construction and professional task correctness. HCD.28 coordinates the needed product contributions; HCD.2 returns an exact missing domain question without inventing its answer.
- **Provider, clinical, safety, legal, employment, specialist, organization, and release owners:** retain their direct competence, participation, authority, and consequence decisions.
