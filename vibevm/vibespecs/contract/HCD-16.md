## HCD.16:1 - Problem Frame {#problem-frame}

**Use this when** one exact Human Capability Development Method or status-preserved candidate and one human-development claim face evidence that can change their disposition for a named repertoire use. The trigger may be a changed learner population, later Work, provider, tool or AI condition, source claim, transfer or retention result, safety boundary, or serious alternative.

Begin by naming the exact Method or candidate, its current status, the claim that can change, and the bounded use for which the repertoire decision matters. The first useful result is a current HCD repertoire slice stating the strongest supported use and its limits. Retaining that use, narrowing a claim, leaving a comparison unresolved, or stopping a use at a real protection limit can finish the present question. When a change or inquiry is justified, assign `adopt`, `revise`, `branch`, `retire`, or a selected `further probe`; insufficient evidence alone does not select a study.

What this buys in practice is local, evidence-bearing change. A practitioner can stop preserving a Method by school loyalty or replacing it by fashion, while keeping Method identity, candidate status, provider conditions, evidence reach, and target-domain authority honest.

Do not use HCD.16 for generic Method recovery, construction, admission, description, variant identity, provenance, trial, fit, worth, introduction, or Method-Engineering culture. Use Method Engineering for those results. Do not open HCD.16 merely to update a bibliography, rebuild a publication, choose one local option, or declare an already-grounded selected set.

## HCD.16:2 - Problem {#problem}

Human-development Methods are often named at the wrong grain. “Coaching,” “deliberate practice,” “apprenticeship,” “retrieval,” or “AI tutoring” can hide different operations, learner conditions, supports, providers, tasks, and evidence claims. A new source, tool version, or local success is then used either to replace the whole repertoire or to preserve it unchanged.

Both reactions lose decision value. A source may contradict only one mechanism claim, one population branch, or one assisted-performance inference. A local failure may concern provider capability, access, assessment, or target-domain correctness rather than reusable Method semantics. Conversely, a reassuring average can conceal a critical error, failed transfer, weak retention, or dependence on support.

HCD.16 keeps the evidence question human-specific while returning Method identity and generic repertoire maintenance to Method Engineering. It changes only the exact HCD claim and bounded repertoire use that the evidence can defeat.

## HCD.16:3 - Forces {#forces}

| Force | Working tension |
| --- | --- |
| Currentness and stability | Respond to material evidence without treating every new publication or tool version as a new Method. |
| Method identity and HCD use | Preserve generic Method semantics and status while deciding whether a human-development use remains warranted. |
| Local evidence and transfer | Use direct observations where they fit without generalizing across learners, Work families, providers, supports, or horizons. |
| Improvement and critical error | Reward useful performance while preventing aggregate gains from hiding unacceptable errors or protected losses. |
| Assistance and human contribution | Use tools, AI, coaching and scaffolding where appropriate while separating equipped performance from later unassisted or changed-support performance. |
| Decision and uncertainty | Finish the strongest supported use or qualified unresolved comparison; select a new probe only for an obtainable contribution worth its full burden. |
| Repertoire and selection | Keep a current field-specific slice without pretending that it performs a local choice, universal retirement, or Method admission. |

## HCD.16:4 - Solution {#solution}

### HCD.16:4.1 - Fix the repertoire use and exact claim {#fix-the-repertoire-use-and-exact-claim}

Name the practitioner or steward, decision owner and authority, bounded repertoire use, target human capability, later-Work family, learner or practitioner population, material conditions, evidence or qualification window, and protected consequences.

Identify one exact HCD Method or candidate and one claim whose disposition can change. A useful claim names the intended action or contribution, population, task or Work, practice and feedback conditions, provider and support, measure, horizon, and inference. “The Method works” is too coarse.

If the repertoire use, subject, status, or claim cannot be recovered, return that missing value. Do not manufacture an HCD disposition from a school name, source citation, carrier, prompt, tool, or local activity label.

### HCD.16:4.2 - Recover Method and candidate status from Method Engineering {#recover-method-and-candidate-status-from-method-engineering}

Recover the Method or candidate status and the reusable semantics needed to identify the present claim. Obtain admission, description, variant lineage, provenance, generic trial, fit, worth, introduction or currentness results from their direct Method-Engineering owners only when this bounded HCD judgement consumes them. Preserve every candidate as a candidate until independent Method admission obtains.

A changed file, source, prompt, support configuration, tool, provider, or local Work deviation does not create a Method variant. When the evidence suggests changed reusable semantics, return the exact changed operation, dependency, entry or stop rule to ME.15. When it concerns description, support, introduction, or performed Work, return it to the corresponding direct result instead.

HCD.16 consumes these results to know what it is evaluating. It neither admits a Method nor retires one universally.

### HCD.16:4.3 - Bind human-development evidence to the correct conditions {#bind-human-development-evidence-to-the-correct-conditions}

For the exact claim, recover evidence about the target action, learner starting state, representative later Work, practice and feedback, distribution and delay, unfamiliar transfer, retention, assessment, provider capability, support and AI conditions, critical errors, burden, and protected consequences at the strength the decision needs.

Keep these evidence states separate:

- assisted practice performance;
- unassisted performance after practice;
- performance under changed or withdrawn support;
- same-job performance after prior assisted exposure;
- unfamiliar same-family transfer;
- transfer to other Work;
- delayed retention under stated intervening practice; and
- provider, cohort, service, organization, or product outcomes.

Evidence for one state may motivate a probe in another; it does not fill it. A provider's capability, a deployed tool's productivity effect, or a learner's corrected answer is not the human capability claim by substitution.

### HCD.16:4.4 - Compare the current claim with serious alternatives {#compare-the-current-claim-with-serious-alternatives}

State the smallest contradiction, missing evidence, or changed condition. Compare at least the current claim, the serious alternative that would change the disposition, and the consequences of retaining, narrowing, branching, or withdrawing the claim for this use.

Ask whether the apparent problem lies in:

| Candidate locus | Decision-changing question |
| --- | --- |
| Source-to-claim row | Does the source actually support this population, task, measure, horizon, transfer, support, provider, or causal inference? |
| Method-use condition | Does the already admitted Method still fit this learner, later Work, provider, support, risk, and evidence need? |
| Population or condition branch | Does evidence defeat only one subgroup, configuration, support posture, or qualification window? |
| Assessment | Did the task, scoring, cue, tool, or missing criterion fail to expose the intended contribution? |
| Provider or access | Was the Method absent, unavailable, or enacted by an unqualified provider rather than defeated? |
| Reusable Method semantics | Did an operation, dependency, entry, stop, or other reusable claim change, requiring an ME.15 return? |

Reopen only the locus that consumes the changed basis. A newer publication date, renamed school, improved interface, or changed carrier does not reopen an unaffected HCD claim.

### HCD.16:4.5 - Settle the current result before selecting further inquiry {#settle-the-current-result-before-selecting-further-inquiry}

First state what the available evidence supports for this repertoire use and which comparison remains unresolved. Retain supported practice, narrow an unsupported claim, or stop a use whose real protection condition is not met. None requires designing a study merely to finish the current account.

Select `further probe` only when a new observation is obtainable in the relevant window and could change the disposition enough to warrant its whole burden. Include designing and describing the inquiry, learner and provider time, preparation, practice, feedback, comparison, delay, specialist access and displaced development or protective Work. A plausible discriminating question does not itself supply people, authority, time or access. Use C.11.DUA when that evidence-demand judgement needs appraisal.

For a selected probe, specify the person's first attempt, allowed help, representative change, source and tool conditions, provider contribution, delay or support contrast when material, critical-error condition, protected contributions, complete burden, stopping rule and decision-changing observations. Keep the target-domain criteria and safety boundary with their owners. An unavailable or unwarranted probe leaves the strongest supported current result and the dependent uncertainty.

A generic Method trial, Method admission, variant decision, or organization rollout remains with ME or another direct owner. HCD.16 selects only the human-development inquiry needed for this bounded repertoire use. Its selection or design is not an obtained observation.

### HCD.16:4.6 - Return the supported slice and any selected change {#return-the-supported-slice-and-any-selected-change}

State the supported current use, narrowed claim, unresolved comparison or real protection stop for the exact HCD repertoire use. For a justified change or selected inquiry, use the following dispositions:

| Disposition | Meaning here | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `adopt` | Include an already admitted Method for the named HCD use under the supported conditions. | Does not admit the Method, select it for one person, authorize Work, or establish effect. |
| `revise` | Change the exact HCD claim, evidence condition, population scope, support condition, or use statement while preserving Method status. | A semantic Method change returns to ME.15; a text edit alone is not the result. |
| `branch` | Keep distinct, evidence-bearing use conditions or populations whose dispositions differ. | Does not create a Method variant or inherit admission for a candidate. |
| `retire` | Stop offering the Method or claim for this bounded HCD use. | Does not erase the Method, source history, candidate account, or other supported uses. |
| `further probe` | Select one feasible, worthwhile observation for the named decision while preserving status and uncertainty. | Selection is not the obtained result. The probe has full burden, access, protection, stop and response conditions; an unavailable later observation withholds only its dependent claim. |

Keep the actual grounds, serious alternatives, uncertainty and change conditions needed by the receiving use in the same repertoire slice. An unresolved comparison is a truthful result when no change or new inquiry is warranted; preserve any independently supported use. Add a probe description only for a selected inquiry. When different claims need different outcomes, keep them separate rather than averaging them into one Method score.

### HCD.16:4.7 - Return the current repertoire slice and route stronger results {#return-the-current-repertoire-slice-and-route-stronger-results}

Retain the positions needed by the current repertoire slice and its receiving use:

| Position | Content for the receiving use |
| --- | --- |
| Use and authority | Practitioner or receiver, bounded HCD repertoire use, decision owner, authority, population, later Work, qualification window, and stop. |
| Subjects and status | Exact admitted Methods and separately status-preserved candidates; current reusable semantics, descriptions and lineage supplied by ME. |
| Human-development claims | Target contribution, learner starting state, practice and feedback, representative Work, transfer, retention, provider, support, safety, tool and AI conditions. |
| Evidence and currentness | Direct observations and sources with population, task, comparator, measure, horizon, support, provider, causal, and currentness limits. |
| Alternatives and result | Serious alternatives, supported current use and limits, unresolved comparison or protection stop; any selected `adopt`, `revise`, `branch`, `retire`, or `further probe`, with scope and protected consequences. |
| Gaps and reopening | Decision-relevant missing values and change conditions; needed return owner and retirement meaning. A selected probe also states its full burden, access, stopping rule and response. |


When several exact already grounded members must be declared for one selector-facing use, pass the members and basis to G.5. When a shipped maintained set, source pack, publication, or other named currentness object needs targeted refresh, use G.11. HCD.16 does not recreate either result for an ordinary claim-sized decision.

## HCD.16:7 - Conformance Checklist {#conformance-checklist}

- The bounded repertoire use, decision owner and authority, population, later Work, qualification window, and protected consequences are explicit.
- One exact admitted Method or status-preserved candidate and one decision-changing HCD claim are named.
- Method Engineering supplies the status, semantics or other result needed by this bounded judgement; unrelated ME evaluations are not prerequisites.
- Assisted, unassisted, changed-support, same-job, unfamiliar-transfer, other-Work, delayed, provider, and organization evidence states remain distinct where material.
- Source population, task, comparator, measure, horizon, support, provider, causal, and currentness limits travel with the claim.
- Serious alternatives and the smallest contradiction or missing evidence are compared without a global Method score.
- The result states the strongest supported use and limits, any unresolved comparison or real protection stop, and any selected change. `further probe` requires an obtainable, worthwhile contribution with full burden, access, protection, stop and response conditions.
- Candidate status is preserved; adoption and retirement do not change universal Method status.
- G.5, G.11, ME, clinical, target-domain, safety, provider, and authority questions are returned only when their own entry conditions obtain.

## HCD.16:9 - Consequences {#consequences}

The HCD repertoire becomes more explicit and easier to repair. Practitioners can identify which Method use remains warranted, which claim changed, which branch needs direct evidence, and which candidate must stay a candidate. Source or tool changes no longer force a global rebuild.

The cost is disciplined evidence binding. Some decisions finish with a limited current use or unresolved comparison; a selected probe consumes real learner and provider resources. A useful local Method can be retired from one use while remaining available elsewhere. Method Engineering, providers, target-domain owners and evidence owners supply only the separate results needed by the particular HCD use.

## HCD.16:12 - Relations {#relations}

Observed HCD.15 arrangement use and human-development evidence may supply a compatible HCD.16 input. A designed arrangement, attendance record, community membership, or provider activity does not establish Method fit, transfer, retention, or worth. Equivalent direct evidence permits independent entry.

ME.15 supplies admitted Method semantics or a status-preserved candidate lineage, provenance, applicability, evidence positions, currentness, and retirement meaning. ME.16 may supply introduction and later-use observations while keeping target, strategy, description, plan, Work, capability, authority, and outside results distinct. HCD.16 returns only the exact contradicted generic claim, edition, support result, or next action to its ME owner.

HCD.1–HCD.14 may supply qualified demand, target, programme, task, support, practice, performance, transfer, retention, and revision evidence. HCD.16 neither reconstructs nor upgrades those results.

A bounded HCD.16 disposition or status-preserved variation account may supply HCD.17 with one cultural-variation input. It does not establish actual enactment, receiving culture, population prevalence, or intervention authority.

G.5 declares a selector-facing shortlist, ranked shortlist, joint-use set, handoff, abstain, or escalation only after exact members and basis exist. G.11 scopes currentness refresh for a named maintained object. C.11 owns an actual local choice. Clinical, safety, legal, employment, provider, organization, platform, learning-product, and target-domain owners retain their Methods, correctness, service, and authority.
